LOST PHONE FINDER - WHAT IS DONE SO FAR
Last updated: 19 October 2026

WHAT THE APP IS
A phone anti-theft app. If your phone is lost or stolen, you can see where it
is - and later you will also be able to ring / lock / erase it from your app.
Built with Expo (React Native) + Firebase cloud.

================================================================================
1. DONE AND WORKING (tested on real phones)
================================================================================

ACCOUNT
 1. Register / login with email + password (Firebase).
 2. "My Devices" list - updates live from the cloud.
 3. Add a phone to your account (name, model, owner phone number).

LIVE LOCATION
 4. "Send location now" button - instant fresh position.
 5. Automatic background tracking: every 10 min or 25 m movement, with a small
    notification. Battery % saved with every fix.
 6. Location history (last 50 fixes) + live map (red = latest, blue = older)
    with Satellite / Streets / Terrain layers. No paid map key needed.
 7. Map shows accuracy circle and fix age; warns if the position is older
    than 3 minutes. Opening the device screen auto-refreshes a stale fix
    (verified live: fresh fix ~6 s after opening, no taps).

THEFT ALERTS
 8. SIM-swap detection: app remembers your SIM at setup; if the SIM changes,
    the phone flips to "LOST" + "SIM swapped" and shows an alert on the phone.
 9. Manual Mark-LOST / Clear-LOST button.
10. SMS composer with prefilled Google Maps link + battery - you just tap send.

OFFLINE MESH (the big one - works when the lost phone has NO internet)
11. The lost phone broadcasts its location sealed & encrypted over Bluetooth.
12. Any other phone with this app can read it and upload it to the cloud.
    The helper phone CANNOT read the location - only you (the owner) can.
13. You tap "Harvest my relays" in your app and the lost phone's location
    appears in your history, marked "mesh".
14. PROVEN end-to-end on 2 real phones, BOTH directions:
    - Samsung beacon + OPPO helper: "checked 5, decrypted 5" -> 5 positions.
    - OPPO offline beacon + Samsung helper: relay landed in history -
      verified directly in the cloud database (the exact location the OPPO
      sealed while it had no internet).
    - Zero-internet proof: the beacon phone ran with Wi-Fi OFF (no internet
      at all) and still worked.
15. Self-healing relay queue: packets read while a helper is offline are
    uploaded automatically later - nothing gets stuck.

PLATFORM / INFRASTRUCTURE
16. Android development build (APK) installed on both test phones.
17. Firebase security rules: only the owner can read their own data/relays.
18. All code committed + pushed to GitHub; project docs kept up to date.
